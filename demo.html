Interactive demo

Explore ProjectVisio with a complete sample portfolio

A fictional ministry with ten projects, every dashboard and report, and an eight-minute narrated guided tour. The demo is available to organisations on request.

Request access

Fill in your details and select the button. Your email app opens with a message to the ProjectVisio team, ready to send. We reply with your personal access code.

Open the demo

Enter the access code from our email. The demo then opens in this browser window.

  • The demo runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you enter in it is sent anywhere, and it resets each time it opens.
  • It is about 6 MB and works best on a laptop or desktop screen.
  • All names, projects and figures in the demo are fictional.
