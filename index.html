About ProjectVisio

Delivery Assured

ProjectVisio is a portfolio oversight dashboard for ministries, departments and agencies that are accountable for delivering public programmes. It brings the schedule, budget, milestones, risks and decisions of every project into one clear, current view, and works as an early-warning system for leadership, so they can act before problems become costly.

It is built for the people who answer for results: Permanent Secretaries, Ministers, chief executives, executive directors and senior managers.

The gaps it closes

  • Scattered information. Updates sit in emails, spreadsheets and memos, so no one sees the whole portfolio at once.
  • Slow, manual reporting. Quarterly reports take days to compile and are out of date when they reach leadership.
  • Warnings that come too late. Slippage in schedule or spending is often found after the deadline has passed.
  • Inconsistent ratings. Each project is judged differently, which makes fair comparison and prioritisation difficult.
  • Decisions that stall. Issues that need an executive decision remain unresolved because they are not visible.
  • Records that disappear. Key documents live in personal inboxes and drives, and leave when staff move on.

Ready for data you are responsible for

Security is part of how every screen works, not an add-on.

  • Separate workspaces

    Every request is limited to the organisation it belongs to. Owners, admins, members and viewers can each do exactly what their role allows, checked on the server for every change.

  • Account protection

    Two-factor sign-in with an authenticator app and backup codes, a list of every signed-in device, and sign-out of any of them. A password change signs out every other device.

  • Encrypted offline copies

    The Windows and Android apps encrypt the data they keep and hold the key in the device's secure storage. Signing out deletes both.

Questions buyers ask

Does my team need to install anything?

No. Everything works in the browser. The Windows and Android apps are optional, for people who need to work offline.

What happens to changes made offline?

They are saved on the device and sent as soon as it is back online. If someone else changed the same item in the meantime, their version is kept and the app tells you which change was not saved.

Can we try it before we buy?

Yes. The Free plan has every feature, limited to five projects per workspace. Upgrade a workspace to Pro when you need more.

Can several departments or clients share one account?

Create a workspace for each of them. People can belong to several workspaces and switch between them, and each workspace keeps its own members, roles and plan.
